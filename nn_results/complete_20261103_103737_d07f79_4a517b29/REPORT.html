Method and data separation

The neural network predicts cable tension from arm state, reference motion, load, shoulder angle, previous force, error integral, and muscle states. Supervised targets are the recorded compensated PID forces. Only accepted, compatible teacher episodes are included. Original train/validation/test assignments are preserved. No time samples from an episode are assigned to another split. Validation selects the checkpoint; test force data are evaluated only after selection. Scaling is fixed using physical units, without fitting on validation or test data.

The 21–64–64–1 tanh network commands cable force directly during OpenSim evaluation, clipped to 0–650 N. The rollout has no PID force fallback. Good force prediction alone does not establish good closed-loop tracking or superiority to the teacher.
